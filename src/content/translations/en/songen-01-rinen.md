**Key points of this article**

1. Questions about people are “**person ↔ keyword**” pairs. Learn the left and right columns of the table as a set.
2. “Independence” (自立／じりつ) does **not** mean “not receiving care”. It means being able to **choose and decide for yourself** (自己選択・自己決定) and to **take part in society** (社会参加).
3. Normalization (ノーマライゼーション) = **bringing life closer to an ordinary life**. Social inclusion (ソーシャルインクルージョン) = **embracing everyone and excluding no one**.

## ① Welfare ideas and the people behind them

### People and keywords

| Person | Keyword |
|---|---|
| **Bank-Mikkelsen** (バンク-ミケルセン, Denmark) | Father of normalization (the 1959 Act) |
| **Nirje** (ニィリエ, Sweden) | The **8 principles** of normalization |
| **Wolfensberger** (ヴォルフェンスベルガー, USA) | **Social Role Valorization** (gaining valued social roles) |
| **Ed Roberts** (エド・ロバーツ) | **Independent Living Movement** (自立生活運動, USA, late 1960s) |
| **Kazuo Itoga** (糸賀一雄／いとがかずお) | “**Make these children the light of the world**” (この子らを世の光に) and **guaranteeing development** (発達保障) |
| **Frankl** (フランクル) | *Man's Search for Meaning* (夜と霧) and **attitudinal values** (態度価値): the attitude you choose when your life is limited |
| **Mayeroff** (メイヤロフ) | ***On Caring*** (ケアの本質): to care is to help the other person grow and realize themselves |
| **Richmond** (リッチモンド) | *What is Social Case Work?* (mother of casework) |
| **Kitwood** (キットウッド) | Person-centred care (パーソン・センタード・ケア, dementia care) |

> **Swapped pairs are a classic trap.** The “7 principles of casework” are by **Biestek** (バイステック), not Richmond. The “ÄDEL reform” (エーデル改革) is not a person's idea but **Sweden's reform of elderly care**.

### How the ideas differ

| Idea | In one phrase | Example of a correct answer |
|---|---|---|
| **Normalization** (ノーマライゼーション) | Bring the **living environment** of people with disabilities close to **ordinary life** | **Moving from institutions to community living**, the Barrier-Free Act, the idea behind the Basic Programme for Persons with Disabilities |
| **Social inclusion** (ソーシャルインクルージョン) | **Embrace everyone as a member of society** and support each other | “A society that **includes** (包摂する) people with and without disabilities” |
| **Independent Living Movement (IL)** (自立生活運動) | Respect **self-determination based on the person's own choices** | Living independently in the community while receiving personal assistance |

**Common wrong answers**: building large residential institutions (the opposite of normalization) / aiming for a complete cure of the disability / functional recovery = independence / “integration” (統合) — inclusion means “**embracing**” (包摂)

## ② Independence, independence support, self-determination and QOL

### What “independence” (自立) means

| Type | Correct description |
|---|---|
| **Mental independence** (精神的自立) | Having goals for your life and **moving things forward as the main actor yourself** |
| Physical independence (身体的自立) | Using your own body to carry out daily life |
| Social independence (社会的自立) | Having a role in society and taking part in it |
| Economic independence (経済的自立) | Living on your own income or assets |

- Independence support (自立支援) = **supporting self-choice and self-determination** (自己選択・自己決定)
- Supporting the person so they can **take part in society** (社会参加) **even while receiving care**
- “Living without anyone's help”, “recovery of ADL” or “focusing on functional training” are ×

### Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Advocacy** (アドボカシー) | **Speaking for** the user's wishes (rights advocacy, 権利擁護) |
| **Empowerment** (エンパワメント) | Drawing out the person's own strength. The empowerment approach **raises the ability to make decisions for yourself** |
| **Universal design** (ユニバーサルデザイン) | **Anywhere, anyone, freely, easy to use**. One of the 7 principles is “**safe** and easy to use” |
| Strengths (ストレングス) | The person's strong points |
| Paternalism (パターナリズム) | Deciding on someone's behalf “for their own good” (父権主義) |

### Raising QOL (quality of life, 生活の質)

- Support not only the physical side but also the **psychological and social sides**
- Decide the plan by looking at the user's **needs and living environment as a whole**
- Use welfare equipment **while discussing it with the user**
- “Improving ADL is a must” and “the same standard for everyone” are ×

## ③ Independence and autonomy, QOL, empowerment

- **Dignity** (尊厳) = the irreplaceable value every person has. It does not change with age or disability. People **cannot always make correct judgements**, and judgement changes with their condition and the situation, so we support them.
- **Katakana terms**: **advocacy = rights advocacy (speaking for the person)** (アドボカシー＝権利擁護) / rapport = relationship of trust (ラポール＝信頼関係) / accountability = duty to explain (アカウンタビリティ＝説明責任) / **QOL = quality of life** (生活の質). **Barrier-free town planning** also helps raise QOL.
- **Normalization** = the idea of guaranteeing **ordinary living conditions** even with a disability. It is not about making the person “normal” but about **arranging the living environment**. Promoted by Bank-Mikkelsen (Denmark), organized into 8 principles by **Nirje**, and developed by **Wolfensberger** into Social Role Valorization (SRV).
- **Independence support** aims for the person to **live with pride and responsibility**. **Having a role** and **building up motivation step by step** raise the will to live. The attitude of **living together with people with disabilities and supporting their independence** is important.
- **“Independence” (自立) vs “autonomy” (自律)**: independence = running your life **by your own will and responsibility**, while also using the services you need. Autonomy = **governing yourself by rules you decided yourself** (the inner ability that underlies independence). “If you receive help, you are not independent” is ×.
- **Social inclusion** (ソーシャル・インクルージョン, 社会的包摂) = excluding no one and embracing everyone as a member of the community. The opposite is **social exclusion** (ソーシャル・エクスクルージョン, 社会的排除).
- **QOL** includes not only “quality of daily life” but also “quality of one's whole life” and “quality of life itself”. The goal of care has shifted **from ADL-centred to QOL-focused**.
- The **empowerment approach** was proposed by **Solomon** (ソロモン) in the USA (1976). It draws out the strength the person already has and supports them to change their own situation.

## Trap patterns (almost always ×)

| Wording in an option | Why it is × |
|---|---|
| Independence = not receiving help from others | Independence with support exists |
| Independence = recovering ADL or function | Self-determination and social participation are central |
| For people with low judgement, put the family's wishes first | Support the person's own self-determination |
| Building large residential institutions | The opposite direction to normalization |
