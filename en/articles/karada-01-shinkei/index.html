How the brain, nerves, autonomic nerves and sense organs work

🌐 This page is a translation for EPA care worker candidates. The national exam is written in Japanese, so key exam terms are shown in Japanese with their reading, like “pressure ulcer (褥瘡／じょくそう)”.

📄 Japanese original：脳・神経・自律神経・感覚器のしくみ

Key points of this article

  1. Cerebrum: “frontal lobe = movement”, “parietal lobe = skin sensation”, “temporal lobe = hearing”, “occipital lobe = vision” (前頭葉・頭頂葉・側頭葉・後頭葉). Amygdala = emotions, hippocampus = memory, medulla = breathing (扁桃体・海馬・延髄).
  2. Sympathetic nerves = the body ready to fight (heart rate ↑, blood vessels narrow, pupils widen, digestion ↓); parasympathetic nerves = the body at rest (digestion ↑) (交感神経・副交感神経).
  3. Cataract = clouding of the lens (白内障＝水晶体の濁り). The ossicles of the middle ear = malleus, incus, stapes (ツチ骨・キヌタ骨・アブミ骨).

① Brain and nerves

Functional localization of the cerebrum (大脳の機能局在)

PartMain functions
Frontal lobe (前頭葉)Motor area (運動野), motor speech area (Broca’s area, ブローカ野), thinking and motivation
Parietal lobe (頭頂葉)Somatosensory area (pain, temperature and touch of the skin) (体性感覚野)
Temporal lobe (側頭葉)Auditory area (聴覚野), sensory speech area (Wernicke’s area, ウェルニッケ野), memory
Occipital lobe (後頭葉)Visual area (視覚野)

Other parts of the brain

PartFunction
Amygdala (扁桃体)Emotions such as fear, anxiety and joy (情動)
Hippocampus (海馬)Memory (remembering new things). Not vision
Cerebellum (小脳)Body balance and adjusting movement
Medulla oblongata (延髄)Breathing centre (呼吸中枢), heart centre, swallowing
Hypothalamus (視床下部)Centre for body temperature, appetite and the autonomic nerves
Pituitary gland (下垂体)Control tower for hormone secretion

Cranial nerves and nerve cells

○×
Trigeminal nerve = sensation of the face (三叉神経)Facial nerve (顔面神経: facial muscles, taste), olfactory nerve (嗅神経: smell), hypoglossal nerve (舌下神経: tongue movement)
Serotonin is a neurotransmitter (神経伝達物質). So are dopamine and acetylcholine—
Glial cells (神経膠細胞) support nerve cellsNerve cells support glial cells / glial cells carry information
Signs that the brain has stopped working = dilated pupils and no light reflex (瞳孔散大・対光反射の消失)Respiratory failure, cardiac arrest, urinary incontinence

37th exam: Mind and Body (こころとからだのしくみ)

Where is the breathing centre?

  1. Cerebrum (大脳)
  2. Midbrain (中脳)
  3. Cerebellum (小脳)
  4. Medulla oblongata (延髄)
  5. Spinal cord (脊髄)
Show answer

Answer: 4　The medulla (延髄) holds the life-supporting centres for breathing, circulation and swallowing.

37th exam: Mind and Body (こころとからだのしくみ)

Which is a sign that the brain has stopped working?

  1. Respiratory failure
  2. Overflow incontinence (溢流性尿失禁)
  3. Cardiac arrest
  4. Weaker peristalsis
  5. Dilated pupils and no light reflex
Show answer

Answer: 5　Pupils that stay wide and do not shrink when light is shone show that the brainstem has stopped working. The three signs of death are respiratory arrest, cardiac arrest and dilated pupils (no light reflex) (死の三徴候).

37th exam: Mind and Body (こころとからだのしくみ)

Which is correct about how the brain works?

  1. Nerve cells support glial cells
  2. Glial cells carry information
  3. Serotonin is a neurotransmitter
  4. The motor area is in the parietal lobe
  5. The hippocampus is responsible for vision
Show answer

Answer: 3　Serotonin is a neurotransmitter. Nerve cells carry information and glial cells support them. The motor area is in the frontal lobe, the hippocampus is for memory, and vision is the occipital lobe.

36th exam: Mind and Body (こころとからだのしくみ)

Which cranial nerve is involved in sensation of the face?

  1. Olfactory nerve (嗅神経)
  2. Trigeminal nerve (三叉神経)
  3. Facial nerve (顔面神経)
  4. Vagus nerve (迷走神経)
  5. Hypoglossal nerve (舌下神経)
Show answer

Answer: 2　Face sensation = trigeminal nerve; moving the facial muscles = facial nerve. Learn them as a pair.

✏️ Try a past exam question (35th exam: Mind and Body)

Which is an effect of the sympathetic nerves?

  1. Blood vessels narrow
  2. Heart rate decreases
  3. Airways narrow
  4. Digestion is promoted
  5. Pupils narrow
Show answer

Answer: 1　The sympathetic nerves are “fight or flight” mode: blood vessels narrow, heart rate rises, airways widen, digestion is suppressed, pupils widen.

Eyes

Disease / changePoint

Ears, nose and speech sounds

○×

38th exam: Mind and Body (こころとからだのしくみ)

Which part becomes cloudy in cataract?

  1. Retina (網膜)
  2. Cornea (角膜)
  3. Choroid (脈絡膜)
  4. Vitreous body (硝子体)
  5. Lens (水晶体)
Show answer

Which is correct about the structure and function of the nose?

  1. The nasal cavity is divided into front, middle and back passages
  2. Nose hairs remove dust
  3. The back of the nasal cavity connects directly to the larynx

④ Brain, hormones, bones and muscles, breathing

  • How the eye works: iris (虹彩) = changes the pupil size to control the amount of light / lens (水晶体) = focuses / retina (網膜) = receives light and turns it into nerve signals. Glaucoma (緑内障) = a disease that damages the optic nerve; raised eye pressure is one cause (parts of the visual field are lost).
  • Visual field problems: concentric narrowing of the visual field (求心性視野狭窄) = you can see the centre but not the surroundings (e.g. retinitis pigmentosa, 網膜色素変性症) / central scotoma (中心暗点) = you cannot see the centre / hemianopia (半盲) = the left or right half of the visual field is missing.
  • The cerebrum is divided into 4 lobes: frontal, parietal, temporal and occipital. The surface (cortex) = grey matter; the inside (medulla) = white matter. Cerebellum = balance and adjusting movement / brainstem (脳幹: midbrain, pons, medulla) = breathing and circulation / hypothalamus = body temperature, appetite and autonomic nerves.
  • The brain weighs about 1,300 g. Medulla = centre for breathing and circulation (“the breathing centre is in the cerebellum” is ×). Cerebellum = balance and adjusting movement (damage causes ataxia, 運動失調). Hippocampus = memory.
  • Hormones: pineal body → melatonin (brings sleep), posterior pituitary → vasopressin (reduces urine = antidiuretic), thyroid → raises metabolism, adrenal medulla → adrenaline (raises heart rate, blood pressure and blood sugar), adrenal cortex → cortisol (deals with stress), pancreatic β cells → insulin (lowers blood sugar), α cells → glucagon (raises it).
  • What bones do: support, protect, move, make blood (bone marrow, 骨髄), store calcium. When a person is bedridden, calcium dissolves out of the bones.
  • Muscles: skeletal muscle = voluntary muscle (骨格筋＝随意筋); smooth muscle (internal organs, blood vessels) and heart muscle = involuntary muscle (不随意筋).
  • The middle ear is connected to the pharynx by the Eustachian tube (耳管), so a cold can easily lead to middle ear infection (中耳炎).
  • The right bronchus is wider, shorter and steeper than the left → swallowed objects easily enter the right lung. The right lung has 3 lobes and the left lung 2.
